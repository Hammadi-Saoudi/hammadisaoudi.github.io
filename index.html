02
Medical AI · 2026

Early Diabetic Retinopathy Detection

Quality-aware deep learning system for diabetic retinopathy grading using the APTOS 2019 dataset. The pipeline combines image quality assessment, CLAHE, Gamma correction, Unsharp Masking, EfficientNet-B3, weighted sampling, label smoothing, augmentation and test-time augmentation. Explainability is integrated through HiResCAM and ScoreCAM.

APTOS 2019 EfficientNet-B3 CLAHE Image Quality HiResCAM ScoreCAM
03
Medical AI · 2025

Explainable Diabetic Retinopathy Diagnosis

Hybrid CNN–Vision Transformer architecture for diabetic retinopathy diagnosis across five severity grades. The system combines Swin Transformer and EfficientNet-B0 with cross-attention, focal ordinal loss and fine-tuning. Grad-CAM is used to provide visual explanations of model predictions.

Swin Transformer EfficientNet-B0 Cross-Attention ViT Grad-CAM Ordinal Loss

Neurodegenerative Disease Classification

Comparative study of deep learning architectures for neurodegenerative disease classification. The project explored ANN, CNN, RNN and LSTM architectures and included an EEG-oriented analysis pipeline involving correlation, feature selection, standardization and classification.

ANN CNN RNN LSTM EEG Feature Selection
